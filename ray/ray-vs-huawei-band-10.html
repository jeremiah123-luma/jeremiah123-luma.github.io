Ray versus HUAWEI Band 10

A thin AMOLED band from HUAWEI, compared with a screen-free band that runs on light.

Andy
Fact-checked August 20, 2026 · 38 independent comparisons
See Ray options from $219

HUAWEI Band 10 is a thin fitness band with a 1.47-inch AMOLED touchscreen and HUAWEI Health insights. It's inexpensive in many markets, with prices that vary by region, and lasts about eight days per charge in typical use. Ray is screen-free, lighter and powered by light.

The short answer

Choose Band 10 if you want workouts, messages and stats on your wrist for a low price. Choose Ray if you'd rather have a quieter, lighter band you never charge.

HUAWEI Band 10 status: Currently sold.
