Ray or Jawbone UP3?

Jawbone UP3 is discontinued. Here's what a current alternative looks like.

Andy
Fact-checked August 20, 2026 · 38 independent comparisons
See Ray options from $219

Jawbone UP3 was a slim, screen-free band known for its design and smart-alarm sleep coaching. It has been discontinued, and its service has ended. Ray is a current screen-free band with more sensors and a supported app, and it powers itself from light.

The short answer

UP3 is only worth researching for its design history. If you want a current screen-free band, Ray is one option.

Jawbone UP3 status: Discontinued / legacy.
