Technology

Solar Power

Our solar power module harvests energy from outdoor sunlight only. About 1 minute of direct sunlight outdoors per day can sustain operation, depending on exposure and use.

Ultra-Low Power Design

We built our own sensors and integrated them with an ultra-low power processor to minimize power consumption while maintaining accurate health monitoring throughout the day.

Advanced Sensors

Ray includes heart rate, temperature, sleep tracking, step counting, and light exposure sensors — all optimized for minimal power consumption without sacrificing accuracy.

Design Philosophy

Health tech shouldn't get in the way of living. The Ray is designed to be small, minimal, and needs no user input to function.

Environmentally friendly — no charging accessories, a longer lifespan than other wearables, and a smaller footprint.

Data privacy and openness — your data is for your eyes only, forever. Leave it, export it, delete it; we won't snoop.

Buy once, own forever — no recurring fees, no planned obsolescence.
