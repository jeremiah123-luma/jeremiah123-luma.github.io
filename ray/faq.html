Yes, the Ray is fully waterproof and can be worn while swimming, showering, spelunking, running, water poloing, or in any weather conditions. Water and band care →

Ray charges using outdoor sunlight only. About one minute of direct sunlight outdoors per day can sustain operation. A full charge provides about 60 days in total darkness, depending on settings and use. How solar charging works →

Indoor lighting barely charges Ray unless you’re right next to a window. It does charge outdoors in shade or cloud, and through the window on a car commute. A full charge lasts about 60 days in total darkness, so a little time outside every so often keeps it topped up. Best light for charging →

Yes. Ray is one of the lowest-EMF trackers you can wear. It charges from light as direct current, so there’s no wireless charging coil and no battery pack charging on your wrist. It uses very little power, its Bluetooth isn’t on all the time, and you can set it to stay off most of the day and sync only when you want your data.
