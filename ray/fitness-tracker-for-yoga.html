Does Ray Track Yoga? Current Activity Support

No yoga tracking yet. Activity features are in development.

Andy
October 25, 2026
See Ray options

Does Ray track yoga?

No. Ray does not currently track yoga. Activity features are in development, but you should not buy Ray expecting a yoga workout tracker today.

Wearing Ray during a class does not mean the app will record that class as a yoga session. Everyday health readings and a dedicated activity record are different things.

What is being worked on?

We’re working on activity features. That is work in progress, rather than an available yoga mode or a promise of a particular release date.

This page will reflect confirmed features as they become available. For now, yoga tracking is not supported.

What about Ray’s other health data?

Ray’s existing health measurements are separate from yoga tracking. You can read the current list of health metrics to see what it records.

Those readings should not be treated as a yoga session log, a count of poses or an assessment of your form.

Should I choose Ray for yoga tracking?

If recording yoga workouts is essential to your purchase, Ray does not meet that requirement today. Wait until the activity features you need are available.

If you’re considering Ray for its existing everyday health features, start with what Ray tracks. For questions about a specific feature, contact us.

Choose Ray based on the features available today.
