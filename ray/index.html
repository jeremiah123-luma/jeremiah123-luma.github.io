How it works

One minute of sun. One day of power

The Ray is missing a charging port, because there's nothing to plug in! A tiny solar cell under the glass keeps it running from the light you already live in. It charges the same way you tan: by going outside.

Go two months without seeing the sun, and it'll still be running when you come back.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does it really never need charging?

Ray charges using outdoor sunlight only. About one minute of direct sunlight outdoors per day can sustain operation. A full charge provides about 60 days in total darkness, depending on settings and use.

How solar charging works →
What if I’m indoors most of the day?

Indoor lighting barely charges Ray unless you’re right next to a window. It does charge outdoors in shade or cloud, and through the window on a car commute. A full charge lasts about 60 days in total darkness, so a little time outside every so often keeps it topped up.

Best light for charging →
Is Ray a low-EMF tracker?

Yes. Ray is one of the lowest-EMF trackers you can wear. It charges from light as direct current, so there’s no wireless charging coil and no battery pack charging on your wrist. It uses very little power, its Bluetooth isn’t on all the time, and you can set it to stay off most of the day and sync only when you want your data. Ray does use Bluetooth Low Energy when it syncs, so it isn’t EMF-free.

Is it waterproof?

Yes, fully waterproof and dustproof to IP68. Swim in it, shower in it, sleep in it. There is no charging port to fail.
