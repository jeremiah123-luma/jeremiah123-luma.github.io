Ray or Evie Ring?

A smart ring designed around women's health, compared with Ray.

Andy
Fact-checked August 20, 2026 · 38 independent comparisons
See Ray options from $219

Evie is a $269 smart ring designed around women's health, including menstrual-cycle context. Its open-gap design can adjust to fingers that swell or shrink, and it needs no subscription. It was listed as out of stock when we checked. It charges every four or more days. Ray is a wristband that powers itself from light.

The short answer

Choose Evie if women's health insights and an adjustable ring fit matter most to you. Choose Ray if you'd rather wear a band and never charge it.
