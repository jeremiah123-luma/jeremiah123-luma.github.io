Before you buy Google Pixel Watch 4

Google's smartwatch, compared with a screen-free tracker you never charge.

Andy
Fact-checked August 20, 2026 · 38 independent comparisons
See Ray options from $219

Google Pixel Watch 4 is a full smartwatch from $349.99, with Wear OS apps, Google services, Fitbit health insights, navigation and safety features. It needs charging about every 30 to 40 hours. Ray has no screen and far fewer features, but it's lighter and powers itself from light.

The short answer

Choose Pixel Watch 4 if you want a smartwatch with Google apps and Fitbit insights. Choose Ray if you want quiet health tracking without a screen or a daily charging plan.

Google Pixel Watch 4 status: Currently sold.
