Ray vs. Motiv Ring

Motiv Ring is discontinued. Here's what a current alternative looks like.

Andy
Fact-checked August 20, 2026 · 38 independent comparisons
See Ray options from $219

Motiv Ring was an early, very compact fitness ring that helped prove the smart-ring category. It has been discontinued, and its service has shut down, so it isn't something to buy new today. If you're looking for a current alternative, Ray is a supported tracker with no subscription, though it's worn on the wrist rather than the finger.

The short answer

Motiv is only worth researching if you're curious about the history of smart rings. If you want a current tracker with no charging and no subscription, Ray is one option.

Motiv Ring status: Discontinued / legacy.
