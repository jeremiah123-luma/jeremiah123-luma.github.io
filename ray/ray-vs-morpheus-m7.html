Ray or Morpheus M7?

A chest-strap training system and an all-day wrist tracker do different jobs.

Andy
Fact-checked August 20, 2026 · 38 independent comparisons
See Ray options from $219

Morpheus M7 is a chest-strap heart-rate monitor sold as a coached training system. You wear it for workouts and a morning recovery test, and Morpheus says it isn't meant for sleep or all-day wear. Ray is the opposite kind of device: a 7-gram wristband you leave on day and night, which powers itself from light.

The short answer

Choose Morpheus if you want daily recovery targets tied directly to heart-rate-zone training. Choose Ray if you want passive, around-the-clock tracking of sleep and everyday health without putting anything on before a test.

Morpheus says the M7 is worn for recovery tests and workouts, not for sleep or continuous all-day wear.

Morpheus M7 status: Currently sold.
