A WHOOP alternative. Powered by light.

Ray is a 7-gram, screen-free wrist tracker that focuses on everyday health signals, rather than WHOOP’s strain and recovery coaching.

Andy
Fact-checked September 8, 2026 · 38 independent comparisons
See Ray options from $219

WHOOP is a screen-free band built around strain, recovery and sleep coaching, and it comes with an annual membership. If you like the idea of a quiet, screen-free tracker but don’t need that coaching, Ray is an alternative: a 7-gram bracelet you buy once, with no membership, that powers itself from light and focuses on everyday health signals instead of training scores.

The short answer

Consider Ray if you want a light, screen-free bracelet for sleep and everyday health tracking, and would prefer to power it with light. Choose WHOOP if strain, recovery scores and established training coaching are central to how you exercise.

Both are screen-free, app-connected wearables. Ray’s health signals do not replicate WHOOP’s coaching system.

WHOOP status: Currently sold.

Ray at a glance

  • Weight: 7 grams, as specified by Chargerless.
  • Power: Light harvesting with outdoor exposure as the practical routine. Chargerless says about one minute of direct sunlight outdoors per day can sustain operation, depending on exposure and use.
  • Dark reserve: Up to about 60 days in total darkness from a full charge, according to Chargerless. This is stored energy, not energy generated without light.
  • What it tracks: Sleep, heart rate, heart-rate variability (HRV), temperature trends, steps and activity, and light exposure.
  • How you use it: Wear the screen-free bracelet and view your health trends in the companion app.
  • Limits: Ray does not provide ECG or WHOOP’s strain and recovery coaching. Shared metric names do not establish equal measurement accuracy.

Why choose Ray

If you want to wear a small bracelet, follow sleep and everyday health trends, and reduce the need to remember a charger, Ray is worth considering. Regular light exposure remains part of using it.

Why choose WHOOP

Common questions

Does Ray need sunlight every single day?

Not necessarily: its stored charge provides a buffer. Chargerless specifies up to about 60 days in darkness from a full charge. The one-minute-per-day claim refers to direct sunlight outdoors. Do not rely on ordinary room lighting; strong daylight next to a window can contribute.

Does Ray have a screen?

No. Ray is screen-free, and you view results in its companion app. WHOOP is also screen-free, so this is a shared feature rather than a Ray-only advantage.

Can Ray replace all WHOOP features?

No. Ray covers everyday signals including sleep, heart rate and HRV, but does not reproduce WHOOP’s strain and recovery coaching or the ECG available on eligible WHOOP MG plans.
