Ray or Oura Ring 4?

Oura Ring 4 is the best-known smart ring. Here's how a light-powered wristband compares.

Andy
Fact-checked August 20, 2026 · 38 independent comparisons
See Ray options from $219

Oura Ring 4 is the best-known smart ring, with a polished sleep and readiness app. It starts at $349, needs a membership after the trial ($5.99 a month or $69.99 a year), and charges on a dock every five to eight days. Ray tracks many of the same signals from the wrist instead of your finger, with no membership and no charger.

The short answer

Choose Oura Ring 4 if you prefer a ring and want Oura's polished sleep and readiness experience, including blood-oxygen sensing. Choose Ray if you want to skip the membership and the charging dock, or you'd rather keep your fingers free.

Oura Ring 4 status: Currently sold.

Common questions

Does Oura Ring 4 require a subscription?

Full Oura app access after the included trial requires membership, currently $5.99 monthly or $69.99 annually in the US.

Does Ray require a subscription?

No. Ray's core app and tracking do not require a recurring plan.

Which is lighter, Ray or Oura Ring 4?

Oura Ring 4 is lighter at 3.3 to 5.2 grams depending on size; Ray weighs 7 grams and is worn on the wrist.

Does Ray include every Oura sensor?

No. Oura includes blood-oxygen sensing; Ray does not. The products also use different form factors and software models.
